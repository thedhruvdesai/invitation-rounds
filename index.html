How a round picks EOIs

Ranked by points

Within each occupation, the highest scores are invited first until the round quota or occupation ceiling is reached.

Ties go to the earliest

At the cut-off score, the EOI with the earlier date of effect is invited first.

60 days to lodge

An invitation gives you 60 days to apply. An EOI stays active for two years.

What this means for your EOI

Bigger 189 rounds

A 25% larger allocation should mean more invitations across 2026–27 and some easing of cut-offs.

Tougher 190 competition

With 491 places down by more than half, regional applicants are likely to redirect to state nomination.

Points test reform

Trade qualifications gain degree-level points. The broader rewrite isn't expected before mid-2027.
